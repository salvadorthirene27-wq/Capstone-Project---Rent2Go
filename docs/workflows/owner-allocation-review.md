# Consigned-car financial allocation review

An agency's configured extra-charge percentages and a renter's accepted assessment do not bind a vehicle owner. After the renter accepts the exact current assessment, the original Cosigner separately reviews applicable extra-charge amounts, conditions, percentages and any service credits reducing original earnings for their vehicle. This module records that agreement or a reasoned dispute. It does not deduct a deposit, collect an additional balance, create earnings, transfer money or complete a booking.

## Workflow

1. An independent agency operator proposes the itemized assessment using approved allocation-policy revisions. The renter accepts the exact current version through the existing assessment workflow.
2. The original Cosigner opens My personal vehicles, opens the relevant vehicle and follows “Review this vehicle’s charge allocations.” The pending list contains current renter-accepted assessments with applicable consigned-car extra charges or service credits and no owner decision.
3. The owner sees only their vehicle's title/dates, original service shares, service revenue after credits, and applicable charge amounts, explanations, policy versions and shares. Mixed-booking services, whole-request totals, refundable-deposit amounts, renter identity/contact, signatures, inspection photos and payment evidence are absent.
4. Acceptance requires a separate explicit checkbox for the exact owner-packet digest and current booking version. An owner cannot accept an assessment they issued as the agency operator. Inactive parties/agencies and other booking stages cannot authorize acceptance. A reasoned dispute records no consent.
5. The decision is immutable. A dispute requires a corrected agency assessment and fresh renter/owner review; it cannot be edited into acceptance. Any newer assessment makes an earlier review historical. Exact retries are idempotent, while changed content under the same key conflicts.
6. The owner's history is independently paginated20 per page. Agency/renter assessment screens show review status. The detailed owner reason is disclosed to the agency operator; the renter receives the status without that private reason.

## Storage and authority

Migration025 adds append-only owner-allocation commands and decisions. A decision binds the request, assessment and vehicle offer to the original owner, exact minimized body/digest, consent, actor and timestamp. Updates/deletes fail through immutable triggers; the runtime role receives SELECT/INSERT only. Migration025 was exercised in the designated isolated database before being applied to the app database. Existing project branch quota prevented a new Neon branch; existing databases, native driver and credentials were preserved. Private consistent application-row exports were saved before/after; managed Auth and object bytes are outside those exports.

The endpoint `/api/vehicles/[id]/allocation-reviews` supports scoped GET and exact PATCH. Reads revalidate the stored owner against the immutable quotation financial snapshot and physical vehicle. Global platform roles and unrelated agency/renter accounts receive no owner-packet access. Writes resolve the parent request server-side, lock it, recheck current agency/actor authority under locks, validate the current assessment/version/digest and preserve command retries. Command bodies cannot provide a different owner, vehicle, allocation or amount. Notifications/events carry a generic review message, never the private owner packet.

The settlement integration guard `assertOwnerAllocationsAccepted` verifies the exact current renter-accepted assessment, saved ownership, active original owner, non-self-issued assessment and matching accepted owner digest. Future financial closing must hold its parent request lock and ordered agency/membership/participant authority locks, including original vehicle owners, throughout posting. It must call this assertion and independently check current source facts, deposits, collections/refunds, service credits, disputes and conserved balances. The assertion is not an authorization or ledger-locking API. Migration026 integration extends this gate to consigned service credits as well as extras. New credit packets disclose only the affected owner’s vehicle and net service reduction. Charge-only legacy packets keep their exact original body and digest. The implemented settlement endpoint uses these decisions under ordered authority locks; see `settlement-accounting.md`.
