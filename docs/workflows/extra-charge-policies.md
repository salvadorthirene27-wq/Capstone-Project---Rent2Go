# Extra-charge allocation policies

Authority: the user's6 November decision that each agency configures allocations by charge type and discloses them for explicit renter acceptance. These settings divide an accepted charge; they neither set a fee amount nor authorize a deduction.

Agency owners configure optional entries in the existing partnership payments/sharing section. Each entry identifies its charge type and service context, review conditions and recipient percentages. Supported rental types are damage, fuel, late return and cleaning; cancellation can apply to rentals or tour-only services. Absence means unconfigured, not a zero charge or an inferred default split.

Draft percentages may remain blank. Submission and independent approval require every percentage to be explicitly set, a100% total, a matching offered service and a single entry for each type/context. Agency-owned rentals cannot allocate to a vehicle Cosigner. Tour-only entries cannot use rental-condition fees or allocate to a vehicle owner/car maintenance reserve. Valid explicit zero is distinct from missing configuration. Decimal input retains up to two places rather than silently rounding invalid precision.

Policy changes follow the existing owner amendment, immutable submission, independent Super Admin review, correction/resubmission and approval workflow. A draft, submitted, rejected or correction-requested revision does not replace the effective approved revision. Owner/reviewer summaries show exact conditions and all percentages before approval. Anonymous profiles retain their existing public projection and omit private allocation settings.

The internal financial resolver returns one exact approved policy with application/revision reference and content digest. It supports the current effective revision or an explicitly selected previously approved revision; a revision that was never approved cannot supply terms. Missing/incomplete/type-inapplicable configuration fails closed. Financial callers still authorize the booking participant and lock agency/booking authority; this helper is not a public permission bypass.

Original vehicle/booking agreements and previous assessment snapshots are not rewritten by a new agency policy. A later financial assessment must preserve the chosen approved allocation, itemized amount and applicable consent. Condition acknowledgement alone never accepts a financial deduction. Consigned-vehicle financial terms must continue to respect owner agreements. Deposits and maintenance reserves remain distinct from earned service shares.
