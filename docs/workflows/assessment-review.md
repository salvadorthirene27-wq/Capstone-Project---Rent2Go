# Itemized assessment review — 7 November 2026

After completed services/current accepted return reports, approved pre-service cancellation, or historical payment reconciliation, an independent agency operator may propose itemized financial terms. Active services, unresolved return conditions, missing ownership snapshots and collections from another quotation fail closed. The original quotation, delivery facts, condition-report references and full collection/refund history are bound to the proposal's source digest. Historical payment reconciliation does not itself establish cancellation fees.

Proposals contain reasoned per-line service credits and explicit extra charges. The server resolves complete approved agency charge-type/context allocations; a client cannot supply policy percentages or alter quotation values. Credits are bounded by the delivered value of their own service. Deposits remain excluded from revenue; original driver-inclusive service values remain intact. Each proposal and decision is append-only, dated and digest-bound.

The renter must explicitly accept the exact pending version or dispute it with a reason. Changed financial/condition facts prevent stale acceptance even after the renter refreshes the booking version. Operators may withdraw pending proposals; disputed/withdrawn proposals require a new revision. A prior accepted revision remains preserved and effective until a replacement is accepted. Owner financial agreement remains an additional prerequisite for consigned-car extra allocations; renter consent does not bind the vehicle owner.

Accepted retained obligations constrain later refund approval in addition to each original collection's capacity. Approval cannot consume the amount retained by the effective accepted assessment. Existing approvals that would over-reserve a newly proposed obligation must be resolved before acceptance. The parent request lock serializes all assessment/refund commands; exact retries do not duplicate events or decisions. Current stored actors and agency authority are rechecked under locks. Private renter assessments are not exposed to unrelated Super Admins, Cosigners or other agencies.

Agency and renter booking pages show exact line items, separate quoted deposits, saved collection/refund figures, policy conditions and percentages, current decision and paginated complete version history. Acceptance is separate from condition acknowledgement. Review errors retain the user's explanation. Demo money labels remain visible. No collection, refund execution, deposit deduction, earnings entry or booking completion is created by this module.
