# Platform commissions and agency remittances

Only immutable PLATFORM allocations from financially closed services create Rent2Go dues. The agreed original allocation is used, including its original policy revision and settlement frequency where recorded. Current policy edits do not retroactively recalculate it. Refundable deposits, Cosigner earnings and maintenance reserves are excluded. A saved weekly/monthly preference does not invent a weekday, deadline or late fee.

## Request, payment claim and receipt

1. A current agency operator reviews the disclosed original sources and selects a positive amount. Each action covers at most twenty sources in the displayed close-date/reference order. The saved request preserves the exact amounts, method, reason and source snapshot. Its reservation prevents another remittance or later revenue reduction from consuming those same dues.
2. Preparing a request reserves dues; it does not confirm payment. The agency can withdraw an unpaid request, or an independent Super Admin can revoke it with a reason. A pending claim or simulated result must be resolved first. Cancelled requests retain their history and release reservations.
3. For a completed external cash/GCash payment, the agency supplies a reference, private PNG/JPEG/PDF proof (up to 5 MB), note and explicit payment attestation. This is an unpaid claim. It creates no receipt until an independent current Super Admin verifies actual receipt, checks evidence and acknowledges the exact amount. Rejection requires a reason and permits a corrected claim. An incoming method/reference can confirm only one receipt; untrusted claims cannot reserve references indefinitely.
4. Simulated card/GCash/PayPal actions use fixed outcomes and never request financial credentials or move money. Success creates a clearly labeled simulated receipt. Pending outcomes remain unpaid until independent platform resolution. Declined/cancelled outcomes retain the unpaid reservation until retry or withdrawal/revocation. Thirty attempts per request bound history; withdrawal remains possible after that limit when no result is pending.
5. A confirmed receipt remains immutable. Its amount and exact source allocations must conserve the original request. No invoice or account debit is fabricated for owner earnings. Requests, claims, review decisions and receipts remain distinct saved records.

Independent platform review excludes anyone who currently owns or actively operates the same agency, even if they also hold the Super Admin role. Current authorization is rechecked after account locks and after private-file storage. Evidence is available only to current authorized agency/platform finance users. Renters and vehicle owners gain no access through their booking or vehicle role. Agency finance access may continue for historical obligations when marketplace activity is disabled; it grants no new publication rights.

## Later refunds and agency acceptance

A separately accepted, posted closed-booking revenue credit may reduce a platform allocation that was already settled. The original remittance receipt is preserved. Its resulting excess is shown separately and does not automatically reduce another service's dues.

The agency receives an exact credit packet containing the original platform source, posted reductions, amounts already settled, remaining excess and future-offset consent. Only a current agency operator can explicitly accept it. Acceptance records an immutable credit grant; it is neither a cash refund nor a payment receipt. A separate acknowledged application consumes available accepted grants and allocates the exact amount to disclosed unpaid platform sources. Funding and destination allocations must each equal the application amount. Different agencies and manual/simulated environments never offset each other.

If a service previously settled using accepted credit is itself reduced later, a fresh excess can arise on that source. It still needs fresh agency acceptance. Earlier grant usage and cash receipts remain unchanged; the reversal cannot manufacture another cash receipt.

Per original source:

- Remaining dues = max(original allocation − posted revenue reductions − receipt-covered amount − applied agency credit, 0) − open reservations.
- Excess = max(receipt-covered amount + applied agency credit − net allocation, 0).

Per agency/environment, accepted credit available equals accepted grants minus actual credit applications. Excess awaiting acceptance equals source excess minus accepted grants. Dues are calculated per source before totals are combined, so an unaccepted excess never silently funds another obligation.

## Concurrency, reports and notices

Platform account locks serialize requests, receipts, credit grants/applications and closed-credit posting. A late credit cannot strand an unpaid remittance reservation; the affected reservation must be resolved or revoked first. Existing owner-account and booking checks remain in force. Optimistic versions, exact source/credit digests and actor-bound retry keys prevent stale or duplicate financial actions. Deferred database guards also enforce source ownership, current authority, conservation and immutable history under the restricted application role.

Agency and platform reports provide separate statements for payment receipts, agency credit acceptances and credit applications, with Philippine-date periods and audited exact-centavo CSV exports. Current balances cover all dates and keep source dues, reservations, accepted carry and unaccepted excess separate. Owner reports exclude these records. Printable saved records preserve the original snapshot and distinguish internal records from official tax invoices or bank statements.

The recipient inbox has a dedicated REMITTANCE category and protected account links. Saved notices are redacted when source access changes. Optional email queues use the same recipient/source checks; adding this category does not enable dispatch, backfill old events or authorize sending email.
