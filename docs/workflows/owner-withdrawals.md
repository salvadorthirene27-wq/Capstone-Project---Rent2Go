# Owner earnings, withdrawals and payouts

A Cosigner withdraws only the original owner allocation posted by a closed financial record. Agency earnings, the Rent2Go allocation, maintenance reserves, refundable deposits, unconfirmed collections and open assessments do not enter this balance. Each account is scoped to one original owner, agency and manual/simulated environment.

## Workflow

1. The owner opens My vehicle earnings and selects the relevant financial records and agency. The statement shows only that owner's vehicle title/service dates, source, agreed owner percentage and earned amount. It excludes renter identity, documents, booking contact and other recipients.
2. The owner requests an exact positive PHP amount within available earnings, chooses a permitted account method and supplies recipient details and a reason. The amount and destination remain immutable. Multiple requests may be queued, but unreviewed requests do not reserve money.
3. An independent authorized agency operator reviews the exact saved request. Approval atomically reserves currently available earnings under the account lock. Two concurrent approvals cannot spend the same available balance. Rejection preserves history and reserves nothing; the owner can withdraw only an unreviewed request.
4. An approved unpaid request remains a reservation. An operator can revoke it and release the reservation once when no pending payout exists. A pending simulation must first be resolved. Approval itself creates no paid record or owner debit.
5. Manual payout is recorded only after execution outside the website. The operator explicitly attests actual payment and uploads private PNG/JPEG/PDF proof, the outgoing reference and a reason. File/type/size validation, preflight, post-storage authority/version rechecks and orphan cleanup protect the upload boundary. No bank or wallet transfer is executed by this application.
6. Simulated GCash/PayPal payouts are separate fixed outcomes. Failed/cancelled simulations preserve the unpaid reservation. Pending outcomes remain unpaid until an agency operator resolves success/failure/cancellation. Owners cannot approve or record their own payout. All simulated screens, statements and printable records stay labeled; no credentials are collected.
7. Successful payout atomically creates one immutable payout record and one equal negative owner-account ledger entry. The approved request is paid exactly once. Original earning entries, requests, reviews, proofs, attempts and decisions stay preserved. A protected printable payout record links back to the appropriate owner/agency account.

## Balances and limits

Available = posted owner earnings minus paid debits minus approved unpaid reservations. Queued requests, reserved approvals and paid payouts are displayed separately. Negative capacity is blocked for payout and requires explicit financial review. This module does not implement post-close refund adjustments or post-payout recovery; existing closed financial facts remain frozen until that compensating workflow is implemented.

Open requests remain visible independently of twenty-row terminal history. Earning statements and withdrawal history are separately paginated. Up to20 open requests and30 payout attempts per approval bound workload; exhausted unpaid approvals can be revoked and freshly requested/reviewed without deleting old history.

Manual and simulated accounts never share capacity. The selected owner/agency/environment cannot be overridden by a command. Payment reference normalization preserves the existing NFKC/whitespace/case policy. Migration028 adds a shared manual outgoing reference registry: the same agency/method/reference cannot be used once for a renter refund and again for an owner payout. Claims are immutable and transactional with their financial record.

## Storage and access

Migration027 adds account versions, append-only commands/requests/reviews/cancellations, payout attempts/resolutions, payout records and owner debits. Deferred constraints require the exact equal debit, account agency/environment and requested method at commit. Runtime access is SELECT/INSERT plus account-version and notification-read columns; account identity and financial history cannot be edited/deleted. Migration028 preserves all existing refund/payout references and claims future manual outgoing transfers across both modules.

Every mutation locks the owner account, agency and current stored participants/membership in the established authority order. Exact retries return the saved result; changed retry content conflicts. Revoked agency access after a waiting lock prevents payout. Historical owners retain their own records when the agency is unavailable and can withdraw an unreviewed request. Independent active agency access is required for review/execution; platform roles and unrelated renters have no private account, payout-record or proof bypass.

API: `/api/owner-accounts`, `/api/owner-accounts/[agencyId]/[ownerId]`, its `/evidence` subroute and `/api/owner-payout-files/[id]`. Personal pages live under `/workspace/earnings`; agency pages under `/agency/[id]/accounting`; protected printable records under `/owner-payouts/[id]`. General website chrome remains consistent, with compact document headers. Native account shortcuts, explicit fields, preserved failed input and disabled stale actions support review.

Payout actions create generic recipient-scoped financial notifications and safe audit metadata transactionally. A unified inbox/email outbox is a subsequent module; no email delivery is claimed. Platform remittance and agency/maintenance accounting reports remain separate from owner withdrawals.
